From this day, the 31st of May, 2026, the mantelis.net website features a working blog!

To be more specific, the website features two blogs: a Lithuanian one and an English one. The entire website is offered in both languages. To change between them, click the two letters ("LT" or "EN") at the top of each webpage.

The content in both languages is bound to be different. Not everything will be translated. The Lithuanian blog might contain essays or more general content, while the English blog will most likely house most of my programming discussion.
